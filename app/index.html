How automatic matching works

Ledger pairs transactions from different accounts when at least one is categorized as Transfer or its description looks like a transfer or account payment, their nonzero amounts are exact opposites, and their dates are within five days. Closest matches are paired once. A transaction’s Budget treatment can always exclude it manually or force it to count normally.
