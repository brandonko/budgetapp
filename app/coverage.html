How to read your coverage

Accounts are grouped by provider, account type, and account name. An old activity date can mean an inactive account, or it can be a reason to check your source and import again.

“Latest recorded import” comes from the newest import timestamp among rows still in your file. It is not a bank sync status. Manual and legacy rows may have no import timestamp. Removed imports cannot be reconstructed from this view.

Every recorded row counts here, including refunded transactions and internal transfers. Coverage never changes transactions or their budget treatment.
