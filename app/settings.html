How pairs are found

Ledger looks for equal and opposite nonzero amounts in different accounts within five days. At least one side must look like a transfer or account payment.

The closest dates are paired first, and each transaction can belong to only one pair.

You review before saving

Nothing changes until you confirm the review. Confirmed flags are saved in your transaction file; page loads never rerun detection.

Imports also propose matches between incoming and existing unmatched transactions. Saved exclusions and Count normally overrides stay unchanged.

Reviewing older data

If you used automatic exclusions before, run this one-time review to preserve those transfers. You can scan again whenever you add or correct older transactions.
