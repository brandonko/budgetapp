How matching works

Ledger checks classifications from top to bottom and uses the first matching rule. Rules have separate matchers for the current category, subcategory, description, account name, and provider. Within a rule, every non-empty regular expression must match. Matching is case-insensitive.

Rules are stored in data/classifications.json.
