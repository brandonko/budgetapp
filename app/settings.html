How pairs are found

Ledger looks for equal and opposite nonzero amounts in different accounts within five days. At least one side must look like a transfer or account payment.

The closest dates are paired first, and each transaction can belong to only one pair.

Possible refunds are credits matching a purchase's exact price within the preceding 90 days. A price match is a suggestion, not proof: choose the purchase before confirming.

You review before saving

Nothing changes until you confirm the review. Durable transaction links are saved in your transaction file; page loads never rerun detection.

For partial refunds or repayments from several people, edit the original purchase and open Refunds & repayments. Refunds and transfers link one pair; repayments can link several credits. The purchase shows their net cost in its original month. Both source amounts stay intact and linked credits are not counted twice.

Imports also propose matches between incoming and existing unmatched transactions. Saved exclusions and Count normally overrides stay unchanged.

Reviewing older data

If you used automatic exclusions before, run this one-time review to preserve those transfers. You can scan again whenever you add or correct older transactions.
