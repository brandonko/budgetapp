About tags & groups

Tags describe recurring interests such as Bike or Tools. A group bundles a specific trip or project, such as Hawaii 2022 or Canyon Aeroad. Each transaction can have many tags but only one group. Use the Group selector above the list to narrow this search, including its totals and category breakdown.

Use Edit multiple to select purchases and assign a group or add tags together. Existing tags named with “(group)” stay as tags until you choose to move their transactions into a group.

Choose Bike and Apparel with Match all to find bike clothing, or Match any to find either interest. Add a category or date filter to narrow the same results. “All time” covers the history you have imported into Ledger.
