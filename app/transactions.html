How this comparison works

Comparison uses all saved transactions in each group and the date range above. Your Transactions search, tags, and account filters are kept separately and do not limit these totals.

Spending is purchases less credit adjustments, excluding transactions marked Refunded or Internal transfer. Income is shown separately, not subtracted from spending. Original amounts remain unchanged.

Groups may span different lengths of time; recorded activity dates are shown for context. All time means the data imported into Ledger, not necessarily a complete purchase history. A dash means no recorded activity in this scope, not zero spending.

About tags & groups

Tags describe recurring interests such as Bike or Tools. A group bundles a specific trip or project, such as Hawaii 2022 or Canyon Aeroad. Each transaction can have many tags but only one group. Choose Group inside Filters to narrow this search, including its totals and category breakdown.

Use Edit multiple to select purchases and assign a group or add tags together. Existing tags named with “(group)” stay as tags until you choose to move their transactions into a group.

Inside Filters, choose Bike and Apparel with Match all to find bike clothing, or Match any to find either interest. Every selection updates the results and totals immediately, together with category, account, and date filters. Each matching transaction is counted once. “All time” covers the history you have imported into Ledger.
