Data import

Bring your data together.

i New to importing? Learn how this page turns account and order history into Ledger transactions.

How importing works

Choose a service tab, complete its fields, and select its Import button. For direct imports, the companion Chrome extension opens the service in your browser and reads data from your signed-in session. Ledger stages the results for review; nothing is written to data/transactions.csv until you select transactions and confirm.

Potential duplicates and $0 transactions appear unchecked in the review. Duplicate matching uses date, amount, and occurrence count, so multiple identical transactions from the same day are still preserved when they represent separate occurrences.

The CSV tab accepts Ledger-shaped transaction files without a createdAt column. Date, description, and amount are required per row; other fields may be blank. Valid rows continue to review even when other rows are invalid.

Transactions that match no saved classification rule are highlighted with a No rule matched badge. Review them manually or use them to identify a classification rule you may want to add.

Credit Karma, Apple Card, and Capital One retain their source categories (or use Uncategorized when unavailable). Amazon, AliExpress, eBay, and Walmart start as Shopping, while Venmo starts as Venmo. Saved classification rules can then assign a matching category and subcategory; otherwise, the initial category is kept and the subcategory remains blank. Generic CSV imports retain the values supplied in each row, including intentionally blank optional fields, before rules are applied.

Dates and account details

Start and end dates are inclusive and limit the transaction or order dates requested by automatic imports. Apple Card CSV uploads review every row in the selected file, so the date fields do not filter a manually uploaded Apple Card CSV. Capital One’s date range applies to both its website import and its downloaded-CSV fallback.

Account name, account type, and provider are labels saved on each imported row—not usernames or credentials. Use account name for the specific card, bank account, or wallet; account type for a broad grouping such as CREDIT CARD or BANK; and provider for the issuing bank or service. You can keep the suggested values or replace them.

Apple Card: automatic importing is currently broken. Export a transaction CSV manually from card.apple.com, then use the Apple Card tab’s CSV upload.

Credit Karma

Imports account transactions and applies expense-oriented signs. Optional filters prevent Amazon, AliExpress, Venmo, eBay, and Walmart entries from duplicating richer source data.

Check your connections first. Disconnected accounts will have missing transactions. Visit Credit Karma Manage Accounts to reconnect any bank or service before importing.

Transaction filters

Turn off Walmart exclusion to include account charges such as membership fees, refunds, or purchases not covered by itemized imports.

Amazon orders

Creates one transaction per item, applies the configured sales-tax estimate, and assigns the payment account selected below.

AliExpress orders

Creates one transaction per order line and reconciles discounts, shipping, and tax to the amount actually charged. Currently supports orders priced in USD.

Venmo

Imports completed payments from Venmo's official statement data. Balance transfers, pending payments, and failed payments are excluded to avoid double counting.

Apple Card

Automatic importing is currently unavailable. Export a transaction CSV manually from card.apple.com, then select it below. Apple Card's categories are retained unless a saved classification rule matches.

Upload an exported CSV

Every transaction in the selected CSV will be reviewed. The date range above applies only to automatic Apple Card imports.

Capital One

Import account transactions from Capital One’s website. Source categories are kept when available; saved classification rules can update them. Purchases count as spending, and credits count as money received.

How this import works

Sign in to Capital One and open the account you want to import. The extension attempts its Download transactions / Export form using CSV. Complete any sign-in or verification yourself; Ledger never receives credentials or cookies.

If the form cannot be filled automatically, select the dates and CSV in Capital One yourself. The extension stays ready to capture the export. If capture is unavailable, use the CSV option below. Website automation still needs verification against your account’s export form.

Dates include both endpoints and filter by Transaction Date, not Posted Date. Include the full requested period in your export. Import one account at a time; set its account name and use BANK for checking/savings. Account and card numbers are not stored.

Credit-card Debit/Credit exports and bank Transaction Amount/Transaction Type exports are supported. An unfamiliar or malformed format stops the import instead of guessing amounts. Overlapping Credit Karma imports still use the normal duplicate review.

Use a downloaded Capital One CSV

Uses the date range and account fields above. Nothing is saved until you confirm the transaction review.

Walmart purchases

Import completed Walmart.com and Walmart+ purchases from your signed-in purchase history. Creates one row per charged item line and shares the receipt total across items, including tax, fees, tips, and discounts.

What is included?

USD receipts available in Walmart purchase history, including linked in-store receipts when available. Dates refer to the original order date, not delivery or bank posting dates.

Cancelled, pending, and returned/refunded orders are reported as skipped in review. Import their charges and refunds from your account instead. Walmart+ membership fees are not an order-history export.

Keep the Walmart tab open while collecting. Sign in or complete any Walmart security check if asked, then retry. Nothing reaches your transaction file until you confirm the review.

eBay purchases

Creates one transaction per purchased item and reconciles each order's total across its items, including shipping, tax, and discounts. Currently supports USD purchases.

Ledger CSV

Imports transactions from Ledger's CSV format. Every valid row is staged for review, and invalid rows are counted without preventing other rows from being imported.

Use this header, without createdAt:
date,description,amount,category,subcategory,accountName,accountType,provider,notes,tags,group,flags

Older Ledger CSV exports without the group column are also supported.

Every row requires a date, description, and amount. All other values may be blank.
