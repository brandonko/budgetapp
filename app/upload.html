Credit Karma

Imports account transactions and applies expense-oriented signs. Optional filters prevent Amazon, AliExpress, Venmo, and eBay entries from duplicating richer source data.

Transaction filters

Amazon orders

Creates one transaction per item, applies the configured sales-tax estimate, and assigns the payment account selected below.

AliExpress orders

Creates one transaction per order line and reconciles discounts, shipping, and tax to the amount actually charged. Currently supports orders priced in USD.

Venmo

Imports completed payments from Venmo's official statement data. Balance transfers, pending payments, and failed payments are excluded to avoid double counting.

Apple Card

Opens card.apple.com and uses Apple's official date-range CSV export. If Apple changes the export page, you can select a previously exported CSV as a fallback.

Import an exported CSV instead

eBay purchases

Creates one transaction per purchased item and reconciles each order's total across its items, including shipping, tax, and discounts. Currently supports USD purchases.
