Data import

Bring your data together.

i New to importing? Learn how this page turns account and order history into Ledger transactions.

How importing works

Choose a service tab, complete its fields, and select its Import button. For direct imports, the companion Chrome extension opens the service in your browser and reads data from your signed-in session. Ledger stages the results for review; nothing is written to data/transactions.csv until you select transactions and confirm.

Potential duplicates and $0 transactions appear unchecked in the review. Duplicate matching uses date, amount, and occurrence count, so multiple identical transactions from the same day are still preserved when they represent separate occurrences.

The CSV tab accepts Ledger-shaped transaction files without a createdAt column. Date, description, and amount are required per row; other fields may be blank. Valid rows continue to review even when other rows are invalid.

Transactions that match no saved classification rule are highlighted with a No rule matched badge. Review them manually or use them to identify a classification rule you may want to add.

Credit Karma, Apple Card, Capital One, and American Express retain their source categories (or use Uncategorized when unavailable). Amazon, AliExpress, eBay, and Walmart start as Shopping, while Venmo starts as Venmo. Saved classification rules can then assign a matching category and subcategory; otherwise, the initial category is kept and the subcategory remains blank. Generic CSV imports retain the values supplied in each row, including intentionally blank optional fields, before rules are applied.

Dates and account details

Settings → Preferences controls the default lookback for every importer (2 weeks initially) and Suggest refund matches. Refund suggestions are enabled by default for all sources, including CSVs. They look for saved purchases at the exact same price in the previous 90 days; you must choose and confirm a match during review. Turning this off disables suggestions everywhere.

Start and end dates are inclusive and limit the transaction or order dates requested by automatic imports. Apple Card CSV uploads review every row in the selected file, so the date fields do not filter a manually uploaded Apple Card CSV. Capital One’s date range applies to both its website import and its downloaded-CSV fallback. American Express browser imports use the selected range. Uploaded Amex files include all dates by default; enable their date-range option to limit rows for review. XLSX includes source categories and optional merchant details in Notes. CSV may contain only date, description, and amount.

Account name, account type, and provider are labels saved on each imported row—not usernames or credentials. Use account name for the specific card, bank account, or wallet; account type for a broad grouping such as CREDIT CARD or BANK; and provider for the issuing bank or service. You can keep the suggested values or replace them.

Apple Card: automatic importing is currently broken. Export a transaction CSV manually from card.apple.com, then use the Apple Card tab’s CSV upload.

Credit Karma

Imports account transactions and applies expense-oriented signs. Optional filters prevent Amazon, AliExpress, Venmo, eBay, and Walmart entries from duplicating richer source data.

Check your connections first. Disconnected accounts will have missing transactions. Visit Credit Karma Manage Accounts to reconnect any bank or service before importing.

Transaction filters

Turn off Walmart exclusion to include account charges such as membership fees or purchases not covered by itemized imports.

Amazon orders

Creates one transaction per item, applies the configured sales-tax estimate, and assigns the payment account selected below.

AliExpress orders

Creates one transaction per order line and reconciles discounts, shipping, and tax to the amount actually charged. Currently supports orders priced in USD.

Venmo

Imports completed payments from Venmo's official statement data. Balance transfers, pending payments, and failed payments are excluded to avoid double counting.

American Express

Import directly from Amex Activity. XLSX keeps source categories and optional merchant details in Notes; CSV starts as Uncategorized without a source category. Saved rules can then assign a category and subcategory.

How this import works

Click Import from Amex to open Amex Statements & Activity. Sign in, choose your card, and click Use this card in the Ledger guide. The companion extension assists recognizable export controls and captures XLSX (preferred) or CSV in your browser. If prompted, set the requested dates and click Download in Amex; no separate upload is needed when capture succeeds.

Requires Ledger Data Importer 0.12.2 or newer. Reload it in chrome://extensions and approve the updated permissions for American Express download detection. After Use this card, wait for Listening for an Amex XLSX or CSV download. If capture fails, expand Capture details in the Amex guide for a privacy-safe status readout, or use Upload a file instead below. Login and security checks stay with you.

Supports US card activity exports with Date, Description, and Amount columns. Purchases are positive in the export; payments and refunds are negative. XLSX uses the Transaction Details sheet, not its summary. Merchant name, address, and extended details become editable, searchable notes when enabled below. Account headers and reference-number columns are not saved.

Browser imports use the inclusive dates below, initially set by Settings → Preferences. Make sure the Amex export covers that whole range. Uploaded files include all dates unless you enable their range option. Use a distinct account name for each card and reuse it for future imports.

Nothing is saved until you confirm the review. Duplicate matching also checks overlapping Credit Karma imports by date, amount, and occurrence count. Import XLSX first to retain its richer details; importing it again does not overwrite existing matches.

Browser import needs companion extension 0.12.2 or newer.

Upload a file instead

XLSX recommended for categories and merchant details. CSV also supported. Maximum 16 MB.

Capital One

Import account transactions from Capital One’s website. Source categories are kept when available; saved classification rules can update them. Purchases count as spending, and credits count as money received.

How this import works

Sign in to Capital One and open the account you want to import. The extension attempts its Download transactions / Export form using CSV. Complete any sign-in or verification yourself; Ledger never receives credentials or cookies.

If the form cannot be filled automatically, select the dates and CSV in Capital One yourself. The extension stays ready to capture the export. If capture is unavailable, use the CSV option below. Website automation still needs verification against your account’s export form.

Dates include both endpoints and filter by Transaction Date, not Posted Date. Include the full requested period in your export. Import one account at a time; set its account name and use BANK for checking/savings. Account and card numbers are not stored.

Credit-card Debit/Credit exports and bank Transaction Amount/Transaction Type exports are supported. An unfamiliar or malformed format stops the import instead of guessing amounts. Overlapping Credit Karma imports still use the normal duplicate review.

Use a downloaded Capital One CSV

Uses the date range and account fields above. Nothing is saved until you confirm the transaction review.

Schwab Checking

Import posted transactions from one Schwab checking account. Choose a distinct account name below, then select that checking account in Schwab.

How this import works

Start the import, sign in to Schwab, and select your checking account. Open its transaction history, choose a range covering the dates below, and export CSV. Keep that tab open; Ledger captures the export and opens a review here.

Import one checking account at a time. Use the same Ledger account name on later imports. Withdrawals are spending; deposits are credits, with classification rules determining their category. Pending transactions are skipped. Brokerage and retirement activity are not supported.

The selected dates include both endpoints. Review duplicates carefully when importing another account or overlapping Credit Karma data: Ledger compares date and amount across accounts. You can select a legitimate transaction marked as a duplicate.

Nothing is saved until you confirm the review. Export account headers, balances, and check-number columns stay in the browser. Ledger receives transaction descriptions but never passwords or cookies. If the export is not captured, cancel and report the issue so the collector can be updated.

Walmart purchases

Import completed Walmart.com and Walmart+ purchases from your signed-in purchase history. Creates one row per charged item line and shares the receipt total across items, including tax, fees, tips, and discounts.

What is included?

USD receipts available in Walmart purchase history, including linked in-store receipts when available. Dates refer to the original order date, not delivery or bank posting dates.

Cancelled, pending, and returned/refunded orders are reported as skipped in review. Import their charges and refunds from your account instead. Walmart+ membership fees are not an order-history export.

Keep the Walmart tab open while collecting. Sign in or complete any Walmart security check if asked, then retry. Nothing reaches your transaction file until you confirm the review.

eBay purchases

Creates one transaction per purchased item and reconciles each order's total across its items, including shipping, tax, and discounts. Currently supports USD purchases.

Ledger CSV

Imports transactions from Ledger's CSV format. Every valid row is staged for review, and invalid rows are counted without preventing other rows from being imported.

Use this header, without createdAt:
date,description,amount,category,subcategory,accountName,accountType,provider,notes,tags,group,flags,id,links

Older Ledger CSV exports without group, id, or links are also supported. Leave id and links blank for new, unlinked rows; Ledger assigns unique IDs.

Exports include linked counterparts, even outside the chosen dates. Import the entire linked set to preserve its net cost.

Every row requires a date, description, and amount. All other values may be blank.
