How importing works

Choose a service tab, complete its fields, and select its Import button. For direct imports, the companion Chrome extension opens the service in your browser and reads data from your signed-in session. Ledger stages the results for review; nothing is written to data/transactions.csv until you select transactions and confirm.

Potential duplicates and $0 transactions appear unchecked in the review. Duplicate matching uses date, amount, and occurrence count, so multiple identical transactions from the same day are still preserved when they represent separate occurrences.

Transactions that match no saved classification rule are highlighted with a No rule matched badge. Review them manually or use them to identify a classification rule you may want to add.

Credit Karma and Apple Card retain their source categories. Amazon, AliExpress, and eBay start as Shopping, while Venmo starts as Venmo. Saved classification rules can then assign a matching category and subcategory; otherwise, the initial category is kept and the subcategory remains blank.

Dates and account details

Start and end dates are inclusive and limit the transaction or order dates requested by automatic imports. Apple Card CSV uploads review every row in the selected file, so the date fields do not filter a manually uploaded CSV.

Account name, account type, and provider are labels saved on each imported row—not usernames or credentials. Use account name for the specific card, bank account, or wallet; account type for a broad grouping such as CREDIT CARD or BANK; and provider for the issuing bank or service. You can keep the suggested values or replace them.

Apple Card: automatic importing is currently broken. Export a transaction CSV manually from card.apple.com, then use the Apple Card tab’s CSV upload.

Credit Karma

Imports account transactions and applies expense-oriented signs. Optional filters prevent Amazon, AliExpress, Venmo, and eBay entries from duplicating richer source data.

Check your connections first. Disconnected accounts will have missing transactions. Visit Credit Karma Manage Accounts to reconnect any bank or service before importing.

Transaction filters

Amazon orders

Creates one transaction per item, applies the configured sales-tax estimate, and assigns the payment account selected below.

AliExpress orders

Creates one transaction per order line and reconciles discounts, shipping, and tax to the amount actually charged. Currently supports orders priced in USD.

Venmo

Imports completed payments from Venmo's official statement data. Balance transfers, pending payments, and failed payments are excluded to avoid double counting.

Apple Card

Automatic importing is currently unavailable. Export a transaction CSV manually from card.apple.com, then select it below. Apple Card's categories are retained unless a saved classification rule matches.

Upload an exported CSV

Every transaction in the selected CSV will be reviewed. The date range above applies only to automatic Apple Card imports.

eBay purchases

Creates one transaction per purchased item and reconciles each order's total across its items, including shipping, tax, and discounts. Currently supports USD purchases.
