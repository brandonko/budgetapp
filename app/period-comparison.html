How this comparison works

Both date ranges include their first and last day. Change means focus period minus comparison. Daily averages divide by every calendar day in each range, including days with no recorded transactions.

Totals follow your dashboard: income is positive; expense credits reduce spending; refunded transactions and excluded internal transfers contribute zero. Unmatched transfers still count normally.

A dash in the category table means no counted transactions in that period. A $0 total can still include activity. Percentages are omitted when the comparison amount is zero or negative. Empty activity is not evidence of a complete bank statement.

Category amounts open the existing Transactions page with this period, category, and spending filters. Period selections stay in this browser so you can return with Back.
