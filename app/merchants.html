How these insights are calculated

Ledger does not store a separate merchant name. Descriptions are grouped only when they match after ignoring case and repeated spaces. Different store IDs and descriptions stay separate; nothing in your CSV is renamed.

Refunded rows and internal transfers contribute nothing. Ordinary negative expense credits reduce spending. The purchase share uses positive charges, and typical purchase is the median positive charge. No activity is different from a zero-dollar net total. Description search and minimum purchase count narrow the ranked list, while headline spending retains the selected dates and category.
